Privacy Policy

For the My School ID Card website, Admin app and Teacher app.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Harsha ID Solutions (“we”, “us”) runs the My School ID Card service: the website myschoolidcard.in, the My School ID Admin Android app and the My School ID Card Teacher app. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who uses our apps

Our apps are business tools for schools, colleges, institutes and organisations (and the staff they authorise) to collect details and produce ID cards. Accounts are created by Harsha ID Solutions or by an authorised administrator. The apps are not meant for use by children.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, mobile number, organisation name and login password (stored encrypted).
  • ID card details entered by schools and organisations: student or member name, class/section or department, roll/ID number, date of birth, blood group, parent/guardian name and phone number, address, photograph and signature, and other fields the organisation chooses to collect.
  • Files you upload: photos (from the camera or gallery), Excel/CSV sheets, ZIP files, ID card templates and logos.
  • Technical information: device type, app version and basic logs needed to keep the service secure and working.

We do not collect your location, contacts, call logs or SMS. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo for an ID card.

3. How we use information

  • To design, check and print ID cards for the school or organisation that provided the details.
  • To let you log in, manage your organisation and send you service messages (for example OTP or password reset).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Sharing

Details are visible only to the school or organisation they belong to, its authorised staff, and Harsha ID Solutions staff who produce the cards. We share information only with service providers who host our servers or send messages on our behalf, under confidentiality, or when required by law.

5. Children’s information

Student details are provided by the school or institute, which is responsible for informing parents/guardians and obtaining any consent required. We use these details only to make ID cards for that school. Parents or guardians may contact the school or us to see, correct or delete a child’s details.

6. Security and storage

All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored on secured servers with access limited to authorised people. Files you download from the apps are saved on your own phone, under your control.

7. How long we keep data

We keep ID card details for as long as the school or organisation uses our service, or as needed to reprint cards. When an organisation or account is deleted, its data is removed from our active systems within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it longer.

8. Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information.
  • Ask to delete your account. See Delete your account.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
