My School ID Card

The Teacher App used by authorized teachers and institute staff to prepare student and member ID cards.

Google Play

What the app includes

Home

Instructions, About Us, products, and best schools.

Templates

Choose the ID card template for the school or institute.

Models

Choose the ID card model.

ID Cards

Open the student or member cards for the organization.

Required Details

The organization logo, signature, and required form are saved on the server for that school or institute.

Photos

Capture a student or member photo, or upload one from the gallery. Pending and captured stay on the current list.

Brochures

Open the brochures uploaded by admin.

Notifications

Admin announcements are delivered in the app.
