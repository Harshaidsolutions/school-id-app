ACCOUNT & DATA DELETION

Delete your account

For My School ID Admin and My School ID Card apps by Harsha ID Solutions.

You can ask us to delete your My School ID Card account and its data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a message to harshaidsolutions@gmail.com or WhatsApp 7799881779 with the subject “Delete my account”.
  2. Include your name, organisation/school name and the email or mobile number you use to log in.
  3. We confirm the request from your registered email or mobile number, then delete the account within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your login account (name, email, mobile number, password).
  • If you are the main administrator of an organisation and ask for the organisation to be closed: its students/members, photos, signatures, templates and uploaded files.

What may be kept

  • Invoices and payment records that the law requires us to keep (usually up to 8 years).
  • Backups, which are overwritten automatically within 30 days.

You can also ask us to delete only some data (for example one student’s photo) without deleting your account, using the same contact details.
