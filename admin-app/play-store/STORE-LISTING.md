## Full description
My School ID Admin is the official app for schools, colleges, institutes and organisations that make ID cards with Harsha ID Solutions.

Everything you do on the My School ID Card admin website, now in an app:

• Schools & institutes – add and manage schools, institutes and organisations
• Students & members – add, edit and search records with photo and signature
• Camera & gallery upload – take student photos directly from your phone
• Bulk upload – import students from Excel/CSV and photos from ZIP
• Crop tool – crop and straighten photos for a perfect card
• ID card templates – upload and manage front/back designs
• ID card generator – preview and generate ID cards
• Parent form links – share data-collection links on WhatsApp
• Notifications, brochures and models
• Downloads – Excel, ZIP and PDF files save straight to your phone

Login is provided by Harsha ID Solutions. Contact us on WhatsApp 7799881779 to get started.
